# Unit group

CRT gives

\[
U(360)\cong U(8)\times U(9)\times U(5)
\cong C_2^2\times C_6\times C_4
\cong C_{12}\times C_2^3.
\]

The exact order distribution is:

| order | count |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 15 |
| 3 | 2 |
| 4 | 16 |
| 6 | 30 |
| 12 | 32 |

The exponent is 12.

A useful exact split over MOD 30 is

\[
1\to K\to U(360)\to U(30)\to1,
\]

where

\[
K=1+(30)=\langle31\rangle\times\langle91\rangle\cong C_6\times C_2.
\]

One explicit (noncanonical) complement is

\[
H=\langle37\rangle\times\langle71\rangle\cong C_4\times C_2,
\]

and reduction maps \(H\) isomorphically onto \(U(30)\).
