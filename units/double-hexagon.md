# The two canonical hexagons

## Power/Frobenius hexagon

\[
Q=U(360)^2=\{1,49,121,169,241,289\}=\langle49\rangle\cong C_6.
\]

## Square-zero congruence hexagon

Because \((60)^2=0\),

\[
C=1+(60)=\{1,61,121,181,241,301\}\cong ((60),+ )\cong C_6.
\]

It is also

\[
C=\ker(U(360)\to U(60)).
\]

Their intersection is

\[
Q\cap C=\{1,121,241\}=G^4\cong C_3.
\]

This is a verified MOD-360 structure. Its best universal characterization and prior-art status remain open.
