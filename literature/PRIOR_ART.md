# Prior-art ledger

This is a living bibliography, not a novelty certificate.

## Regular residues

László Tóth, **“Regular integers modulo n”**, arXiv:0710.1936 (2007).

Relevant point: regular residues modulo a prime power are controlled by the zero/unit local alternatives, and the counting formula yields

\[
\varrho(360)=(\varphi(8)+1)(\varphi(9)+1)(\varphi(5)+1)=175.
\]

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.1936

## Integral / GCD circulant graphs

Wasin So, **“Integral circulant graphs”**, *Discrete Mathematics* 306 (2006), 153–158.
DOI: 10.1016/j.disc.2005.11.006

Relevant point: circulant spectra are obtained from roots-of-unity sums; GCD-defined connection sets lead naturally to Ramanujan sums. Therefore the 24 GCD-relation/Fourier layer is classical background, not a novelty claim.

URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2005.11.006

## Finite Frobenius-ring duality

Jay A. Wood, **“Duality for modules over finite rings and applications to coding theory”**, *American Journal of Mathematics* 121 (1999), 555–575.
DOI: 10.1353/ajm.1999.0024

Heide Gluesing-Luerssen, **“Partitions of Frobenius rings induced by the homogeneous weight”**, *Advances in Mathematics of Communications* 8 (2014), 191–207.
DOI: 10.3934/amc.2014.8.191; arXiv:1304.6589.

Relevant point: finite residue rings \(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z\) are standard examples of finite Frobenius rings. The character module identifies with the ring, annihilator duality satisfies the double-annihilator property, and character-theoretic orthogonality gives the familiar size/duality relations. Therefore M360-021 is a concrete specialization, not a general novelty claim.

URLs:
- https://doi.org/10.1353/ajm.1999.0024
- https://doi.org/10.3934/amc.2014.8.191
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.6589
