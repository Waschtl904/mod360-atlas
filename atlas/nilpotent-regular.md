# Nilpotent and regular skeleton

## Nilradical

\[
N=(30),\qquad N^2=(180)=\{0,180\},\qquad N^3=0.
\]

The largest square-zero ideal is

\[
(60),\qquad (60)^2=0.
\]

## Regular residues

For a prime power \(p^a\), the regular residues are \(0\) together with the units. Hence CRT gives

\[
M=(\{0\}\cup U(8))\times(\{0\}\cup U(9))\times(\{0\}\cup U(5)),
\]

so

\[
|M|=(1+4)(1+6)(1+4)=175.
\]

For MOD 360 this set is exactly

\[
M=\operatorname{im}(x\mapsto x^{13})=\operatorname{Fix}(x\mapsto x^{13}).
\]

Every residue admits the exact decomposition

\[
x=r+n,\qquad r=x^{13}\in M,\quad n=x-r\in N,\quad rn=0.
\]

## Support idempotents

For every \(x\), \(x^{12}\) is idempotent. The eight values are

\[
0,1,81,136,145,216,225,280.
\]

Their CRT coordinates are the eight Boolean support patterns in \(\{0,1\}^3\) for the local factors modulo \(8,9,5\).
