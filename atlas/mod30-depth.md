# MOD 30 coarse geometry and the 12-fold depth

Let \(R=\mathbb Z/360\mathbb Z\). The nilradical is

\[
N=(30)=\{0,30,\ldots,330\},\qquad |N|=12.
\]

Since \(\operatorname{rad}(360)=30\),

\[
R/N\cong\mathbb Z/30\mathbb Z.
\]

Thus every MOD-30 class has exactly 12 lifts to MOD 360:

\[
r,\ r+30,\ldots,r+330.
\]

For a unit \(u\), the fiber has two simultaneous descriptions:

\[
u+(30)=uK,
\]

where

\[
K=\ker(U(360)\to U(30))=1+(30).
\]

### Depth multiplication

Write an element of \(K\) as \(1+30j\), \(j\in\mathbb Z/12\). Then

\[
(1+30j)(1+30k)=1+30(j+k+6jk)\pmod{360}.
\]

Hence the additive depth coordinate is \(j+k\), while multiplicative depth is the twisted law

\[
j\star k=j+k+6jk\pmod{12}.
\]

The twist is exactly the contribution of \((30)^2=(180)\).
