# Additive Fourier structure of GCD relations

For each divisor \(d\mid360\), define the translation-invariant relation matrix

\[
A_d(x,y)=1_{\gcd(x-y,360)=d}.
\]

The additive characters

\[
e_k(x)=\exp(2\pi i kx/360)
\]

diagonalize every \(A_d\). The eigenvalue at frequency \(k\) is the Ramanujan sum

\[
\lambda_d(k)=c_{360/d}(k).
\]

Thus the same divisor lattice that organizes spatial difference types also organizes spectral degeneracies through \(\gcd(k,360)\).

This belongs to the classical theory of GCD/integral circulant graphs and Ramanujan sums. The research question here is not its novelty, but how it interacts with the **multiplicative** character decomposition of \(U(360)\).
