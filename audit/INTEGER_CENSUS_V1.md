## Attack these points

1. Exclusion of integer 0, and special treatment of integer 1 and primes 2,3,5.
2. Inclusive bounds and unequal counts at the ends of MOD-360 columns.
3. `omega` versus `Omega`, squarefree including 1, proper powers excluding primes.
4. Semiprimes: avoid counting distinct `pq` twice and squares `p^2` zero/twice.
5. Sieve congruence: use `360^-1 mod p`, not `p^-1 mod 360`; require `n >= p^2`.
6. Semiprime formula: there the inverse is instead `p^-1 mod 360`.
7. Distinguish exact finite counts, elementary all-bound identities, and novelty.
8. No conversion of one-window count differences into an asymptotic preference.
