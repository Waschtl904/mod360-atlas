# Cyclotomic/Frobenius interpretation of the square hexagon

Let

\[
L=\mathbb Q(\zeta_{360}),\qquad
\operatorname{Gal}(L/\mathbb Q)\cong U(360)=G.
\]

Let

\[
H_2=\ker(g\mapsto g^2)=G[2],\qquad |H_2|=16.
\]

Then

\[
G/H_2\cong C_6.
\]

The fixed field is

\[
F_6=L^{H_2}=\mathbb Q(\zeta_9)^+\,\mathbb Q(\sqrt5),
\]

a degree-6 abelian extension with Galois group \(C_6\).

The identification with the square image is via

\[
G/H_2\to G^2,\qquad [g]\mapsto g^2.
\]

For primes \(p\nmid360\), the Frobenius class in \(\operatorname{Gal}(F_6/\mathbb Q)\) corresponds under this identification to

\[
p^2\pmod{360}\in Q.
\]

This gives the square hexagon a genuine Frobenius interpretation. It does **not** by itself supply any RH/Weil positivity mechanism.
